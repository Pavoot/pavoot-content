## How X is different from LinkedIn
- **Shorter and faster.** A tweet is one punch. If it needs a story, make it a thread.
- **Casual, human, conversational** but with **normal capitalization** (Ana does NOT want
  all-lowercase posts). Contractions, fragments, dry humor all good.
- **The first line is the entire ballgame.** No "I'm excited to announce." Open on the sharpest thing.
- **No hashtags.** They're dead on X and look try-hard. Zero.
- **Emojis: rare.** Maybe one. Usually none.
- **Spicier than LinkedIn.** Opinions, light shade, contrarian takes travel further here. Still
  never punch at our buyers (field marketers) or trash a tool we build on top of (Luma).
- **@-mentions matter.** Tagging @ycombinator, @Luma, etc. can extend reach — but only when it's
  natural and flattering, never bait. Be careful tagging companies in shade.
- **Threads** for stories/build-in-public: a killer hook tweet, then 3–8 short tweets, each able to
  stand alone. End with a soft line or a question. Don't number unless it helps.
- **Build-in-public is the native genre.** Numbers, honesty, "shipped this today," the grind. Founders
  who share the real process get followed.
- Length: default to fitting ~280 chars per tweet even if premium allows more — tighter hits harder.

## Cadence and mix (from founders who grew on X)
- **Volume wins on X, unlike LinkedIn.** 1–2 posts a day tends not to work. Aim for **2–4 a day per active account**,
  plus replies. To find what works fast, run an **intense week of ~6 posts a day** once, then settle at 2–4.
- **Mix, roughly thirds:**
  1. **X-native**: quick takes, observations from the room, build-in-public numbers (only [PUBLIC] ones), threads,
     and replies / quote posts on big accounts' posts in the founders' lane (YC, GTM, events, AI companies).
  2. **Repurposed video/photo**: short clips or photos from events and talks (also for Instagram/YouTube); real
     footage only.
  3. **Cross-posted updates**: the day's LinkedIn post rewritten for X (shorter, spicier), Reddit lessons, launches.
- **One idea, many channels**: every strong idea should become a LinkedIn post, an X take or thread, a Reddit
  discussion (rewritten for Reddit, no product), and an Instagram carousel when it has steps. Do the 80/20 on each
  channel: double down on whatever format is working there.
- **Replies count as posts**: 3–5 thoughtful replies a day on posts in the lane grow an account faster than
  broadcasting. Add something (a number, a counterpoint, an experience), never "great post".
