# Posting strategy — which account, which platform

**Core principle: personal accounts are the engine, the company account is the amplifier.
Don't replace one with the other — run both, with clear roles.**

## Why personal >> company page
- LinkedIn's algorithm favors people over logos. A founder's post reaches far more people than the
  same post on a company page (often many times more). People follow, trust, and root for humans.
- For a Demo-Day push aimed at investors, customers, and hires, **80–90% of the value lives on
  Ana's and Gohar's personal accounts.** A company page strips out the face and voice that make a
  post work.
- Never publish a post ONLY on the company page. Founders post first; the page amplifies.

## What the company page (Pavoot) is for
- Reshares founder posts (small reach bump, builds the page feed).
- Official record: when someone googles Pavoot and clicks the page, it shouldn't be empty —
  logo, tagline, recent posts, follower count.
- Hard announcements (launch, funding, big hire) where a brand voice fits.

## Account roles
| Account            | Role                                                        | Effort |
|--------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|--------|
| Ana (personal)     | Primary. Founder story, traction, technical takes.          | ~40%   |
| Gohar (personal)   | Primary. Strategy, hot-takes, traction. Cross-amplifies Ana.| ~40%   |
| Pavoot (company)   | Amplify + official record. Reshares founders, announcements.| ~20%   |

Both founders posting = double the personal reach, and you cross-amplify each other (see below).

## LinkedIn rules
- Max **1 post per profile per ~24h** (a second same-day post cannibalizes the first).
- "2 a day" is fine only as **1 from Ana + 1 from Gohar** — stagger themes so it doesn't look coordinated.
- Best windows: **Tue–Thu, 8–10am** for traction/hot-takes/vision. Weekends for warm story posts.
- Spread posts across the whole run to Demo Day — don't front-load and go quiet.

## Cross-amplification (cofounder)
1. When one posts, the **other comments early** (first hour, substantive) — biggest reach lever.
2. Optional **plain repost** (no commentary) — cheap extra exposure, won't hurt your own post.
3. **"Repost with thoughts"** acts like a new original — only on a day you're NOT posting your own.
4. Family/friends: supportive but low-value for reach unless their network overlaps with our ICP
   (founders, investors, event/marketing/sales). Don't make them the distribution plan.
   Cofounder comments and peer engagement (YC batchmates, customers) are what move the needle.

## X strategy
Same hierarchy — **founders first, @pavoot supporting** — with two differences:
- **@pavoot can be looser/funnier** on X than a LinkedIn company page. Personality travels.
- **Volume is fine on X.** No same-account cannibalization penalty. Post several times a day,
  reply inside others' threads, quote-tweet relevant news (e.g. the Anthropic raise). X rewards
  being active in the conversation, not just broadcasting.

## Bottom line
Push hardest on **Ana + Gohar personally** on both platforms. Let **@pavoot amplify**. On X
specifically, lean into higher volume and replies.
