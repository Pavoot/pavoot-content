# Swoogo vs Pavoot (researched 2026-09-28)

Sources: swoogo.events homepage, Capterra / Software Advice listings. Pricing is from third-party listings, not Swoogo directly.

## Swoogo in one line
Mature event management platform (registration, websites, logistics) that sells on ease of use and in-house support. Tagline: "eliminate event busywork, and give you time back for the human stuff."

## Facts about Swoogo
- Features: registration flows with rules/logic, event websites, email marketing, session and speaker management, sponsor management, mobile apps (Go Attend, Go Onsite check-in), cross-event reporting, 30+ native integrations + APIs, virtual/hybrid streaming.
- AI: main visible AI feature is an **MCP server** that exposes event data to the customer's own AI tools. No AI agent running the event itself.
- Segments: conferences, **field marketing**, internal corporate events, webinars. Tech, media, manufacturing, higher ed.
- Customers: OpenAI, The New York Times, Docusign, GoFundMe, Justworks, Verkada, Alaska Airlines.
- Scale: 50M attendees managed, 4.9/5 on G2.
- Pricing: from ~$11,800/year per user (Professional), Enterprise custom. Unlimited events and registrations, priced per seat.

## Where they differ
| | Swoogo | Pavoot |
|---|---|---|
| Core job | Run the logistics of an event (register, website, check-in, sessions) | Get the right people in the room and turn conversations into pipeline |
| Model | Software a human operates | AI agent that does the work |
| Who's in the room | Collects whoever registers | Sources and picks the right attendees |
| Emails | Email marketing tools, templates + logic | Every registration email personalized per attendee, host's voice, sent from host's inbox, reply-to a real person |
| During event | Check-in app, attendee app | Captures on-floor conversations (notetaker) |
| After event | Attendee analytics, reporting | Top leads, personalized follow-ups, CRM sync, pipeline attribution |
| Sweet spot | Big conferences, multi-track, sponsors, hybrid | In-person customer events: dinners, meetups, field marketing events |
| AI | MCP server over event data | AI is the product |
| Pricing | ~$11.8k/user/yr | (not public) |

## Where Swoogo is clearly ahead
Maturity and breadth: sponsor and speaker management, mobile apps, virtual/hybrid, 30+ integrations, 50M attendees of track record, big logos. Pavoot should not pitch itself as a Swoogo replacement for a 5,000-person multi-track conference.

## Overlap / risk
Both sell to field marketing teams. Swoogo already has the "give you time back for the human stuff" message, which is close to ours. Our difference has to be concrete: who's in the room, and what revenue came out of it.
