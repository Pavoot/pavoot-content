## 0. Truth rules (break one and the draft is useless)
- **Granola summaries are often wrong about who said or owns what.** They regularly credit another company's
  pitch, event or numbers to Pavoot (and the reverse). Never use a fact from a meeting summary unless you checked it
  in that meeting's transcript (get_meeting_transcript) AND it is clearly about Pavoot. When in doubt, leave it out
  or put it under "Needs before posting" as a question.
- **Never draft an invite or announcement for an event unless it is confirmed as Pavoot's own**: a founder dropped
  it as a Content idea, or `guidelines/pavoot-today.md` lists it as confirmed, or it is in the Pavoot app as our
  org's event. Events in calls with prospects or partners are THEIR events or proposals.
- **No invented specifics**: no made-up dates, venues, guest counts, quotes, customer names or results. If a post
  needs one, write `[needs: …]` in the text and list it under "Needs before posting".
- **News facts need a public source link** you actually opened (newsjacks especially).
- Before writing the files, re-read every factual sentence and ask: which source proves this, and is it about
  Pavoot? Remove anything you can't answer.
